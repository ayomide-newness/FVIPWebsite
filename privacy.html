FISH VALLEY INVESTMENTS & PROPERTY (FVIP) PRIVACY POLICY

Effective Date: 7 September 2026

1. INTRODUCTION

Fish Valley Investments and Property Limited ( RC-1135640) of Apt. 7B, Shonny Park Place, 2A Shonny Highway, Shonibare Estate, Maryland, Lagos, Nigeria. ("Fish Valley", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, process, and share your personal data when you interact with our website, visit our experience centers, or engage with our sales and marketing channels. We operate in strict compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

Fish Valley is the data controller of the personal data described in this Policy. We also comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Act General Application and Implementation Directive 2025 (GAID), issued by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

2. THE DATA WE COLLECT

We collect different types of data depending on your interaction with us:

2.1. Identity & Contact Data

Personal Information: Name, Title, Marital Status, Date of Birth.

Contact Details: Email address, Phone number, Residential address.

Identification: Government-issued ID (NIN, Driver's License, International Passport) required for KYC and Site Access.

Biometric Data: Where you attend our site, a facial image or fingerprint template captured for access control. Biometric data is sensitive personal data under section 30 of the NDPA and we process it only with your explicit consent.

2.2. Financial & Transaction Data

Payment Details: Bank account details and payment proofs (for transaction verification only; we do not store full payment card numbers or card security codes).

Purchase History: Details of units reserved, payment plans selected, and contract status.

Source of Funds: Information required for Anti-Money Laundering (AML) compliance.

2.3. Technical & Usage Data

Digital Footprint: IP address, browser type, device information, and approximate location derived from your IP address. We do not collect precise geolocation from your device without your consent.

Behavioral Data: Pages visited, time spent on the website, and click-through rates from our marketing emails.

2.4. Profile & Marketing Data

Preferences: Your interest in specific unit types (e.g., "3-Bedroom Apartment"), investment budget, and timeline.

Consent Status: Your preferences for receiving marketing communications (Opt-in/Opt-out).

3. HOW WE COLLECT YOUR DATA

We use the following methods to collect data:

Direct Interactions: When you fill out a "Book a Viewing" form, subscribe to our newsletter, or physically visit our Sales Centre.

Automated Technologies: As you interact with our website, we may automatically collect Technical Data via cookies and pixels (e.g., Meta Pixel, Google Analytics). Cookies and pixels that are not strictly necessary for the website to function are set only after you have given consent through our cookie banner, and you may withdraw that consent at any time.

Third Parties: We may receive data about you from authorized Real Estate Brokers, Financial Partners (Mortgage Banks), or public sources (Credit Bureaus).

Where we obtain your personal data from a third party rather than from you, we will tell you that we hold it, and the source, within a reasonable period after obtaining it. We will only request a credit report about you with your prior written consent.

4. PURPOSE OF PROCESSING (WHY WE NEED YOUR DATA)

We will only use your personal data for the following purposes:

Sales & Transaction Management
Contractual Necessity: To process your reservation, issue Offer Letters, and manage the Sale & Purchase Agreement (SPA).

Marketing & Communications
Consent: To send you updates on new phases, construction progress, or investment offers (only if you have opted in).

Site Security & Access
Consent and Legitimate Interest: To control access to the physical Fish Valley site and ensure safety. Where access control uses biometric data, we rely on your explicit consent under section 30 of the NDPA, and a non-biometric alternative (photo identification and gate pass) is available on request.

Legal Compliance
Legal Obligation: To comply with SCUML (EFCC) reporting requirements for Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and KYC.

Service Improvement
Legitimate Interest: To analyze customer demographics and improve our website, product offerings, and customer experience. We carry out a legitimate interest assessment before relying on this basis, and you may object to this processing at any time under section 36 of the NDPA.

5. DISCLOSURE OF YOUR PERSONAL DATA

We do not sell your personal data. However, strictly for the purposes outlined above, we may share your data with:

Authorized Brokers/Agents: If you were introduced by a specific broker, we share deal progress updates with them to calculate commissions.

Financial Partners: Mortgage banks only upon your specific request to facilitate financing.

Service Providers: Trusted vendors acting as data processors (for example, customer relationship management providers and facility managers), each engaged under a written data processing agreement as required by Article 34 of the GAID. A current list of our processors is available on request.

Government & Regulatory Bodies: The EFCC (SCUML), Lagos State Lands Bureau, or Tax Authorities, where required by law.

6. DATA SECURITY

We have implemented appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used, or accessed in an unauthorized way.

Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit (SSL) and at rest.

Access Control: Only authorized employees with a business "Need-to-Know" have access to your personal data.

Vendor Vetting: We engage every third-party service provider under a written data processing agreement and assess the technical and organisational measures they apply to your data.

Breach Response: We maintain a documented breach response procedure and a breach register. Where a personal data breach is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the NDPC within 72 hours of becoming aware of it, as required by section 40 of the NDPA, and will notify you directly where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to you.

8. YOUR LEGAL RIGHTS

Under the NDPA, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

Right to Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Right to Correction: You can ask us to fix inaccurate or incomplete data.

Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can ask us to delete your data where there is no good reason for us to continue processing it (e.g., you are not a debtor or current title holder).

Right to Withdraw Consent: Where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time under section 35 of the NDPA, including by unsubscribing from marketing emails. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before you withdrew.

Right to Object: You can object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing (section 36, NDPA).

Right to Restriction: You can ask us to restrict processing while a question about the accuracy or lawfulness of that processing is resolved.

Right to Data Portability: You can ask to receive the personal data you provided to us in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, or to have it transmitted to another controller (section 38, NDPA).

Rights in Relation to Automated Decisions: You have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing, including profiling, which significantly affects you (section 37, NDPA).

Right to Lodge a Complaint: You can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission at any time (Article 39, GAID). We ask that you contact our Data Protection Officer first so that we have the opportunity to resolve the matter.

We will respond to any request within one month of receiving it. We may ask you to verify your identity before we act on a request, and we do not normally charge a fee.

9. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

Details of the safeguards we apply are available from our Data Protection Officer on request.
